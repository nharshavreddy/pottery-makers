Our Story

PotSmith Artizians
Private Limited

PotSmith Artizians Private Limited was founded by Prashanth Kumar Ramancol and Praveen Kumar Thurupu with a shared vision: to redefine spaces through the timeless beauty of handcrafted pottery and artistic planters.

Born out of a deep passion for clay artistry and sustainable living, PotSmith specializes in manufacturing premium-quality pots, planters, and bespoke clay art pieces. Today, we are proud to stand as one of the largest manufacturers in the Hyderabad region, catering to homes, corporate offices, commercial spaces, and large-scale landscaping projects.

We believe that every environment can be transformed by the warmth and elegance of clay. Our creations harmoniously blend rich tradition, meticulous craftsmanship, and contemporary design to bring your spaces to life.
